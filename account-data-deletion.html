Astro Fighter – Account & Data Deletion

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

This page explains how users of the Astro Fighter mobile application can request deletion of their account and associated personal data, where applicable.

1. Request Account Deletion

If Astro Fighter provides an account or online profile feature, you can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us using the email address below.

Send your deletion request to:

Request Account Deletion

When sending the request, please use the email address associated with your Astro Fighter account, if applicable, and include enough information for us to identify the relevant account.

2. What Data May Be Deleted

Depending on the features you use, an account deletion request may result in deletion of information associated with your account, such as:

  • Account or profile information.
  • Game-related account information.
  • Online game progress associated with the account.
  • Leaderboard or online profile information, where applicable.
  • Other personal information directly associated with the account.

3. Data That May Need to Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained when required by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, or other legitimate legal purposes.

Where information is required to be retained, it will be kept only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

4. Deleting the App

Uninstalling Astro Fighter from your Android device removes the application from your device, but it may not automatically delete account information stored by online services.

If you have an account or online profile, use the account deletion request above to request deletion of associated data.

5. Guest Users

If you use Astro Fighter without creating an account, there may be no server-side account associated with you. Locally stored game data may be removed by uninstalling the application or clearing the application's data through Android settings.

6. Processing of Deletion Requests

We will review account and data deletion requests and take reasonable steps to process valid requests.

Additional information may be requested when necessary to verify that the request relates to the correct account and to prevent unauthorized deletion.
